# 💫 About Me:
Hello, my name is Zaed Hussain, and I am an AI Engineer with a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Illinois Chicago and a Data Engineering Certificate from the University of Chicago. I specialize in building production-ready AI applications and systems powered by Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), agentic AI, and deep learning.

My expertise spans LLM-powered applications, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, document chunking, AI agents, tool calling, and single- and multi-agent workflows. I develop end-to-end AI systems that integrate machine learning models, retrieval pipelines, vector search, external tools, APIs, and agent orchestration to deliver reliable, scalable AI applications. My data engineering background also enables me to build the data pipelines and infrastructure that support AI systems, from data preparation and processing to retrieval and integration.

I work with Python, PyTorch, LangChain, LangGraph, LangServe, LitServe, Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK), and other AI frameworks to build, evaluate, and deploy AI applications. Drawing on my background in applied mathematics and data engineering, I apply mathematical reasoning, algorithmic principles, and optimization techniques to understand and improve the design, performance, and reliability of AI systems.
